Remote Control Car Project

PHASE_01 // ARCHITECTURE_&_MATERIALS

Project Scope & Bill of Materials

Me and my friend like thingies that move, so it becomes natural for us to build a RC car. This project fulfilled our interest and also improved our problem solving and creative thinking, especially in the engineering field. It also enhanced our hands-on experience with tools.

The RC car is controlled via an 27 MHz 4CH RC module (transmitter and receiver), with a wood chassis, plastic cover and used double batteries to power 2 DC motors. Through this project, we explored circuit wiring, steering design, sketching, experimenting design and troubleshooting hardware problems.

Outcome: A fully functional RC car capable of forward/reverse driving and turning maneuvers.

PHASE_03 // TRIAGE_&_DIAGNOSTICS

Hardware Challenges & Troubleshooting

During the build process, we encountered structural and electrical hurdles. Each issue required analytical tracing and iterative prototyping.

⚡ Sub-System: Steering Actuation

  • Problem Ident: Steering motor unable to break free of static friction constraints to rotate assemblies under weight.
  • Root Cause Analysis: The 2 AA battery cell configurations lacked the voltage capacity required to step up motor output.
  • Correction Path: Soldered two dual-cell battery holders together in series configuration. This supplied 6V to max out the motor potential, clearing the issue successfully.

🖥️ Sub-System: Main Receiver Power Drop

  • Problem Ident: RF Receiver stack fails to boot entirely upon toggle.
  • Root Cause Analysis: Resistance check isolated an unstable connection at the primary V+ trace terminal point.
  • Correction Path: Desoldered and laid down a clean structural bead across the V+ line, checking insulation with secure heat shrink tubing wrappers.
