FromSoftware Bookshelf

PHASE_01 // SCOPE_&_IDEATION

Overview & Brainstorming

I bought about 1000 yuan (212 AUD) worth of FromSoftware Books, and I need a bookshelf for it. At the start, I was browsing online for cool bookshelves, but all of them were either too complicated for my old, not-so-high-quality second-hand Ender 3 Pro, too small, or I just didn't like the style.


I ended up designing a really simple bookshelf that is big enough to fit all my books. The bookshelf is split into two halves, with a simple joint mechanism to connect the two. Through this small project, I developed critical thinking through measurements and designing, and I also had an introduction to tolerance and joint mechanisms.

PHASE_02 // PROTOTYPING

Joint Mechanism Engineering

I took inspiration from a random bookshelf design on MakerWorld. It uses a specific joint mechanism, which I adapted into my bookshelf to be a lot simpler.
