PHASE_01 // COMPONENT_HARVEST

Sourcing & Initial Circuit Mapping

Before launching straight into the physical chassis build, the component parameters had to be completely locked down and vetted on a breadboard array. The goal was maintaining clean, decoupled power links.

Acquired Hardware Manifest

  • Microprocessor: Arduino Uno Board configuration core (ATmega328P).
  • Actuation Blocks: Dual 6V high-torque brushed yellow geared DC motors.
  • H-Bridge Isolation Driver: L298N module module to handle raw inductive reverse EMF.
  • Telemetry Nodes: HC-SR04 ultrasonic transducer transceiver module.

Engineering Notes

Initial desk validation flagged major electrical noise. When connecting both motor modules onto the structural logical ground line without optocoupler isolation, sudden acceleration steps caused heavy logic drops inside the ATmega controller core, forcing random restarts. Solved this early by dedicating separate power line rails for logic and raw motor tracking blocks.

PHASE_02 // STRUCTURAL_FAB

Chassis Construction & Drivetrain

With basic components verified, work shifted into framing up the physical mobile footprint of the vehicle assembly.

Structural Assembly Notes

  • Secured the twin DC motor modules to the bottom clear acrylic baseplate tracking line.
  • Aligned rear trailing caster wheels to optimize quick multi-directional spin transitions.
  • Positioned the L298N driver board centrally to maintain uniform copper tracing distances across both drivetrain terminals.

Everything was centered properly to maintain stable weight management during quick pivot rotations when correcting directional tracking around tight obstacles.

PHASE_03 // MECHATRONIC_SENSING

Sensory Rig & Servo Sweeper Setup

An autonomous rover cannot re-route correctly if it only monitors a fixed, narrow tunnel directly ahead. This build phase added dynamic environmental awareness.

Integration Milestones

  • Mounted an SG90 micro-servo directly down onto the front structural nose block.
  • Aligned sensor bracket to set home coordinate calibration precisely to 90 degrees (Dead Center alignment).
  • Interfaced the HC-SR04 bracket directly onto the servo spline assembly horn.

Spatial Geometry Mechanics

By pairing ultrasonic depth measuring routines alongside rotational sweep scripts, the vehicle can survey a wide arc area without changing its primary footprint path vector alignment.

State Logic Protocol

When the center sonar sweep flags an object closer than the danger line threshold, the firmware triggers a brief tracking freeze sequence. It spins right and left vectors sequentially, computes which field coordinate offers maximum path vacancy, and shifts motor tracks to that heading.
